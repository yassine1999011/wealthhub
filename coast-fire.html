Coast FIRE Strategy: Work Less Now, Retire Fully Later

Published in 2026 • 6 min read • By WealthHub Editorial Team

What if you didn't have to wait until traditional retirement age to enjoy your work-life balance? Coast FIRE is a variation of the financial independence movement that allows you to downshift to low-stress work much earlier in life.

What Exactly is Coast FIRE?

Coast FIRE means you have saved enough money in your retirement accounts early in life so that, assuming historical market returns, your investments will grow on their own to cover your full retirement expenses by age 65 without you adding another dime.

Once you reach this milestone, your only financial obligation is earning enough to cover your current, day-to-day living expenses.

How to Calculate Your Coast FIRE Target

To find your Coast FIRE number, you work backward from your traditional retirement target. If you need $1,500,000 at age 65, and you are 30 years away, compound growth does the heavy lifting for you based on an average 7% inflation-adjusted return.
