Coast FIRE Strategy: Work Less Now, Retire Fully Later

Published in 2026 • 10 min read • By WealthHub Editorial Team

Traditional early retirement often demands an exhausting sprint of extreme frugality and massive savings rates for 10 to 15 straight years. But what if there was a liberating middle ground? Enter Coast FIRE—a powerful financial strategy that allows you to downshift your career intensity decades ahead of schedule by letting compound interest do the heavy lifting.

1. What Exactly is Coast FIRE?

The core premise of Coast FIRE is simple: you front-load your retirement savings early in your working life. Once your portfolio reaches a specific threshold (your "Coast number"), compound growth takes over entirely. Assuming historical market returns, your existing investments will grow on their own to cover your full living expenses by standard retirement age, without you ever needing to add another dollar of principal.

Once you hit this milestone, the psychological pressure vanishes. Your only remaining financial duty is earning enough income to cover your current, day-to-day living expenses. You can step down from high-stress corporate roles, switch to part-time consulting, or pursue lower-paying passions without endangering your future financial security.

2. The Mathematics Behind the Coast Number

Calculating your Coast FIRE target requires working backward from your traditional retirement age. It depends heavily on three variables: your target nest egg at age 65, your current age, and the assumed annual investment return (typically adjusted for inflation around 7% for US index funds).

For example, if you estimate you will need $1,500,000 at age 65, and you are currently 30 years old with 35 years of compounding ahead of you, your required Coast FIRE capital today is dramatically lower than your final retirement target—often only a fraction of the total amount.

3. Why Coast FIRE Changes Everything for US Workers

In the modern American economy, burnout is real. Traditional FIRE requires keeping your foot pressed firmly on the gas pedal for decades. Coast FIRE introduces a profound lifestyle upgrade:

  • Eliminating Burnout: You no longer have to endure toxic work environments just to maximize your 401(k) contributions.
  • Flexibility and Autonomy: Freedom to experiment with entrepreneurship, freelancing, or seasonal work.
  • Compound Interest Advantage: The earlier you start investing aggressively, the sooner compound growth takes over your financial future.
