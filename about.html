The Story Behind the Data

Hi, I'm Yassine Idrissi, the creator behind WealthHub. With a background rooted in data analytics and a deep, personal passion for financial markets, crypto, and trading platforms, I've spent years analyzing how numbers shape our financial reality.

I realized that most people don't need more complex financial jargon or expensive advisors to reach financial independence. They need clear, actionable data. Whether you are aiming for early retirement (FIRE), building a robust S&P 500 portfolio, or optimizing your tax strategies through an HSA, the math remains the same.

That is exactly why I built WealthHub: to translate complex financial concepts into simple, interactive calculators. By bridging the gap between raw data analytics and everyday personal finance, my goal is to give you the clarity needed to make the best financial decisions for your future in the United States.
